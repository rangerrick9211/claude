## Decisions
- Wrap the live pages; no standalone rebuild.
- One app with three tabs; not three separate apps.
- Tab switcher at the top, because Basecamp and Family have their own bottom bars.
- claude.ai's slim header stays. Hiding it would mean posing as Claude's desktop app.
- Repo is public, so the offline Almanac copy (GPS traces, due date) stays out of git. Cloud builds ship without it.
- Signing: archive unsigned and let Apple's cloud certificate sign the upload. This avoids a new development certificate on every run. A fallback signs the archive normally and revokes the certificate it creates.
- Ruled out: an official Claude "install artifact on iPhone" feature (found none), and controlling Travis's Mac from a cloud chat (not possible).
